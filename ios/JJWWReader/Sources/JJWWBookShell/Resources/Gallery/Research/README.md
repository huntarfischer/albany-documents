# JJWW Research Gallery Drop

Put research images for *Jesse James and the Widow Whipple* in this folder and push them with the branch.

Supported image formats:

- PNG
- JPG / JPEG
- HEIC
- TIF / TIFF

Images placed here, including inside additional subfolders, are bundled with the app and automatically appear in the developer Editorial Gallery as **researchImage / UNPLACED**. You do not need to edit the manifest just to collect or preview research.

When an image is chosen for the authored reader, promote it into `../editorial-gallery-manifest-v0.1.json` with its Gallery-relative filename, metadata, insertion style, and eventually its exact canonical placement.

Examples of Gallery-relative filenames:

- `Research/cherry-hill-exterior.jpg`
- `Research/maps/albany-1827.png`
- `Research/portraits/elsie-whipple.jpeg`

The image folder is a research reservoir, not an ordering system. Do not infer reader placement from filenames or subfolders. Image breaks remain explicitly authored against the canonical text once the full edition is mapped.
